# 🏴‍☠️ My Favorite Anime: ***One Piece***

> A boy made of rubber sets sail to become the King of the Pirates.

---

## 📺 About the Anime

***One Piece*** is a pirate adventure anime made by **Toei Animation**.

| Detail         | Info                          |
|----------------|-------------------------------|
| Genre          | Adventure, Action, Comedy     |
| Studio         | Toei Animation                |
| First Aired    | 1999                          |
| Main Character | Monkey D. Luffy               |

## 🗺️ The Story

1. Gol D. Roger, the King of the Pirates, hid his treasure called the **One Piece**.
2. **Luffy** eats the **Gum-Gum Fruit** and his body turns into rubber.
3. He gathers a crew and sails the **Grand Line** to find the treasure.

## ⚡ Luffy's Powers

- **Gear 2** – super speed
- **Gear 3** – giant body parts
- **Gear 4** – bouncy, powerful form
- **Gear 5** – his strongest, most cartoon-like form
- **Haki**
  - Observation Haki
  - Armament Haki
  - Conqueror's Haki

## ⭐ Why I Like One Piece

It has **amazing fights**, *emotional moments*, and the **best friendships** 😄.
Luffy looks ~~silly~~ **funny**, but he is ***serious when his friends need him***.
